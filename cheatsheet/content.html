Stability certificates §2

Lyapunov function on $U \ni x^*$

  • (D1) $L$ continuous
  • (D2) $L(x^*) = 0$,  $L(x) > 0$ for $x \neq x^*$
  • (D3) nonincreasing along orbits: $t_0 < t_1 \Rightarrow L(\varphi_{t_0}(x_0)) \ge L(\varphi_{t_1}(x_0))$  (strict: never $=$)

differentiable case: $\frac{d}{dt} L(\varphi_t(x)) = \nabla L \cdot f \le 0$ (Lie derivative)

Lyapunov criterion  &  LaSalle

$\exists$ Lyapunov function $\Rightarrow$ stable (sublevel sets $S_\varepsilon$ are positively invariant)

$L$ not constant on any nontrivial orbit $\Rightarrow$ asymptotically stable ($L$ is constant on $\omega(x)$, so $\omega(x) = \{x^*\}$)

$\omega$-/$\alpha$-limit sets

$\omega(x) = \{y : \exists t_j \to +\infty,\ \varphi_{t_j}(x) \to y\}$,  $\alpha(x)$: $t_j \to -\infty$

First integral constant of motion

$\nabla g \cdot f \equiv 0$.  Hamiltonian: $x' = \partial_y H$, $y' = -\partial_x H$ has $H = \tfrac12 |y|^2 + V(x)$ as first integral (not a Lyapunov function in general).

harmonic oscillator: $L = x^2 + y^2$ is both

Linear(ized) stability

$x' = Ax$: all $\operatorname{Re}\lambda_j < 0 \Rightarrow$ asympt. stable; some $\operatorname{Re}\lambda_j > 0 \Rightarrow$ unstable.

maps $x_{k+1} = Ax_k$: multipliers $|\mu_j| < 1 \Rightarrow$ asympt. stable.

$\operatorname{Re}\lambda_j = 0$ cases undecided by linearization $\to$ center manifold

Worked example: 1D bifurcation analysis exam pattern

$x' = f(x,p) = px + 2x^2 - x^3 = x\,(p + 2x - x^2)$

  • steady states: $x = 0$ and $x_\pm = 1 \pm \sqrt{1 + p}$ (exist for $p \ge -1$)
  • linear stability: $\partial_x f = p + 4x - 3x^2$; at $x = 0$: $\lambda = p$ — stable $p < 0$, unstable $p > 0$
  • fold at $(x,p) = (1,-1)$: $f = 0$, $\partial_x f = 0$, $\partial_{xx} f = -2 \neq 0$, $\partial_p f = 1 \neq 0$  ✓
  • transcritical at $(0,0)$: $x_-$ crosses $0$, stability is exchanged
  • sketch: branches $x = 0$ and the parabola $p = x^2 - 2x$ in the $(p,x)$-plane, solid = stable, dashed = unstable

$\lambda_{1,2} = a_{1,2} + i b_{1,2}$ eigenvalues of $Df(x^*)$; qualifiers: stable/unstable by $\operatorname{sign} a_j$.

Hyperbolicity & invariant manifolds §3, §10

flows: $x^*$ hyperbolic $\iff$ $Df(x^*)$ has no eigenvalue with $\operatorname{Re}\lambda = 0$
maps: $x^*$ hyperbolic $\iff$ all multipliers $|\mu| \neq 1$ of $Dg(x^*)$

Invariant splitting

$E^s = \{y : e^{tA}y \to 0,\ t \to +\infty\}$,  $E^u$: $t \to -\infty$;  $\R^d = E^s \oplus E^u$ with projections $P^s, P^u$.

Stable/unstable manifold theorem flows & diffeos, $f \in C^k$

$W^s_\loc(x^*) = \{y \in U : \varphi_t(y) \in U \ \forall t \ge 0,\ \varphi_t(y) \to x^*\}$

$W^{s/u}_\loc$ are $C^k$ manifolds, tangent to $E^{s/u}$ at $x^*$; globalize by $W^{s/u} = \bigcup_t \varphi_t(W^{s/u}_\loc)$.

constructions: Lyapunov–Perron (variation of constants + contraction) or Hadamard graph transform; maps: $W^s_\loc = \{y: g^k(y) \to x^*\}$

ex. Duffing $\ddot x = x - px^3$: saddle at $0$, $\dim E^s = \dim E^u = 1$; Arnold cat map on $\T^2$: every point hyperbolic

Hartman–Grobman

$x^*$ hyperbolic $\Rightarrow$ $\varphi_t$ is locally topologically conjugate to the linearization $e^{tA}$, $A = Df(x^*)$. Maps: $h \circ A = g \circ h$, $h = \Id + H$ unique via Banach fixed point.

$\Rightarrow$ near hyperbolic points, the linear picture is the whole local picture (up to homeomorphism — see equivalence ladder)

Non-hyperbolic? — center manifolds

Spectral split

$d_c$ eigenvalues $\operatorname{Re}\lambda = 0$, $d_s$ with $\operatorname{Re}\lambda < 0$, $d_u$ with $\operatorname{Re}\lambda > 0$; $E^c = $ span of critical eigenvectors.

Center manifold theorem $f \in C^k$

$\exists$ local $C^k$, $d_c$-dim invariant manifold $W^c_\loc(0)$ tangent to $E^c$; solutions staying near $0$ for $t \to \pm\infty$ lie on it.

caveats: not unique, possibly not analytic/not $C^\infty$ — e.g. $x' = x^2,\ y' = -y$ has the family $y = p\,e^{1/x}$ $(x<0)$

Reduction theorem

With blocks $x' = Bx + F(x,y)$, $y' = Cy + G(x,y)$ ($x \in \R^{d_c}$): locally topologically equivalent to

$x' = Bx + F(x, H(x)), \qquad y' = Cy$

where $W^c_\loc = \operatorname{graph} H$, $H = \mathcal{O}(x^2)$.

Recipe: computing $W^c$ exam workhorse

  • shift equilibrium to $0$, split into $(B, C)$ blocks (diagonalize)
  • ansatz $y = h(x) = a_2 x^2 + a_3 x^3 + \dots$
  • invariance equation $\;\mathcal{N}(h) = Dh(x)\,[Bx + F(x,h)] - Ch - G(x,h) = 0$
  • match coefficients; approximation thm: $\mathcal{N}(h) = \mathcal{O}(|x|^p) \Rightarrow H = h + \mathcal{O}(|x|^p)$

ex. $x' = xy,\ y' = -y + px^2$: $a_2 = p$, $W^c: y = px^2 + \mathcal{O}(x^4)$, reduced flow $x' = px^3 + \mathcal{O}(x^5)$.

Poincaré map at $y \in \Gamma$

Multipliers & monodromy

multipliers $\mu_1, \dots, \mu_{d-1}$ = eigenvalues of $DP(0)$ — independent of the point on $\Gamma$, the section, and coordinates (local Poincaré maps are conjugate).

variational equation $u' = Df(\Gamma(t))\,u$; monodromy matrix $M(T)$ has eigenvalues $\{1, \mu_1, \dots, \mu_{d-1}\}$.

all $|\mu_j| < 1 \Rightarrow \Gamma$ asymptotically stable.

Suspension & obstruction

$g(x) = -\tfrac12 x$ is not a time-$T$ map of any flow on $\R$ (orientation). But every diffeo $g$ of $\R^d$ is the return map of a flow on the $(d{+}1)$-dim mapping torus $M = (\R^d \times [0,T])/\!\sim$, $(g(x),0)\sim(x,T)$.

Hyperbolic sets §7

Hyperbolic set $g \in C^1$ diffeo, $\Lambda$ compact invariant

$\exists\, C > 0$, $\lambda \in (0,1)$ and subspaces $E^s(x), E^u(x) \subset T_x M$:

  • (D1) invariance: $Dg_x E^{s/u}(x) = E^{s/u}(g(x))$
  • (D2) $|Dg^j_x v| \le C\lambda^j |v|$ on $E^s$, $j \ge 0$
  • (D3) $|Dg^{-j}_x v| \le C\lambda^j |v|$ on $E^u$
  • (D4) splitting: $T_x M = E^s(x) \oplus E^u(x)$

Anosov diffeomorphism: $\Lambda = M$.  ex. cat map $A = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$ on $\T^2$.

Shadowing

$\delta$-pseudo-orbit: $d(g(x_k), x_{k+1}) \le \delta$. Near a hyperbolic $\Lambda$: $\forall \varepsilon\ \exists \delta$ such that every $\delta$-pseudo-orbit is $\varepsilon$-shadowed by a true orbit.

numerics with rounding errors still trace real orbits

Persistence

$C^1$-small perturbations $f$ of $g$: nearby compact invariant sets remain hyperbolic, and $\exists$ hyperbolic $K$ with $\psi \circ f|_K = g|_\Lambda \circ \psi$, $\psi$ homeo near $\Id$.

Cor: Anosov diffeos are open in $C^1$ and structurally stable.

the horseshoe's invariant set is hyperbolic $\Rightarrow$ chaos persists → see the road to chaos

Structural stability §6

Definition

$f \in C^1(M)$ is structurally stable if $\exists \varepsilon > 0$: every $g$ with $\|f - g\|_{C^1} < \varepsilon$ generates a flow topologically equivalent to $\varphi_t^f$.

$\|f\|_{C^k} = \sum_{|\alpha| \le k} \sup_K |D^\alpha f|$ on compact $K$ — a Banach space

Nonwandering set

$x \in \NW(\varphi)$: every neighborhood $U \ni x$, $\forall t_0\ \exists t > t_0$: $\varphi_t(U) \cap U \neq \emptyset$.

Peixoto compact 2-manifold

$f \in C^1$ structurally stable $\iff$

  • (T1) finitely many equilibria & periodic orbits, all hyperbolic
  • (T2) no saddle–saddle connections: $W^s(x) \cap W^u(y) = \emptyset$
  • (T3) $\NW(\varphi) = \{\text{equilibria}\} \cup \{\text{periodic orbits}\}$

$M$ orientable $\Rightarrow$ structurally stable fields are open and dense in $C^1(M)$. Fails for $d \ge 3$.

ex. torus flow $x' = \omega_1$, $y' = \omega_2$: structurally unstable ($\omega_1/\omega_2 \in \Q$: all orbits periodic; irrational: all orbits dense)

Morse–Smale system

(D1) finitely many equilibria + periodic orbits, all hyperbolic; (D2) all $W^s \cap W^u$ transversal; (D3) $\NW$ = equilibria $\cup$ periodic orbits.

Thm: Morse–Smale flows on compact $M$ are structurally stable (any dim) — but not generic for $d \ge 3$.

Generic property

holds on a residual set = countable intersection of open dense sets.

ex. hyperbolicity is generic in $\R^{d \times d}$

The road to chaos §14, §15

Periodically perturbed Hamiltonian system

$x' = f(x) + \varepsilon g(x,t)$,  $f = (\partial_{x_2} H, -\partial_{x_1} H)^{\!\top}$, $g$ $T$-periodic; unperturbed homoclinic orbit $\gamma(t)$ to a saddle $p$. Autonomize on $\R^2 \times S^1$, take the stroboscopic Poincaré map $P_\varepsilon^{t_0}$ — the saddle persists: $p_\varepsilon^{t_0} = p + \mathcal{O}(\varepsilon)$.

↓leading-order splitting of $W^s$, $W^u$

Melnikov function

$M(t_0) = \displaystyle\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} f(\gamma(t - t_0)) \wedge g(\gamma(t - t_0), t)\, dt$

signed distance: $d(t_0) = \varepsilon\, \dfrac{M(t_0)}{|f(\gamma(0))|} + \mathcal{O}(\varepsilon^2)$,  $a \wedge b = a_1 b_2 - a_2 b_1$.

simple zero of $M$ $\Rightarrow$ $W^s(p_\varepsilon) \pitchfork W^u(p_\varepsilon)$; no zeros $\Rightarrow$ no intersection.

Duffing $x'' = x - x^3 + \varepsilon(\alpha \cos t - x')$, $\gamma_\pm = \pm(\sqrt2\,\mathrm{sech}\,t,\ -\sqrt2\,\mathrm{sech}\,t \tanh t)$: $M(t_0) = -\tfrac43 + \sqrt2\,\alpha\pi\,\mathrm{sech}(\tfrac{\pi}{2}) \sin t_0$ — simple zeros for $\alpha$ large enough

↓transverse homoclinic point $q \in W^s(0) \pitchfork W^u(0)$

Smale–Birkhoff homoclinic theorem

diffeo with hyperbolic saddle + transverse homoclinic point $\Rightarrow$ some iterate $g^\kappa$ has an invariant set $X$ conjugate to a horseshoe.

$\lambda$-lemma: a disk $D$ transverse to $W^s$ has iterates $g^n(D)$ accumulating $C^1$ on $W^u$ — this folds a rectangle near $q$ into a horseshoe. (Transverse homoclinic points occur only in maps / Poincaré maps, never in planar flows.)

↓stretch $\mu > 2$, contract $\lambda < \tfrac12$, fold

Smale horseshoe  $\cong$  full shift

$X = \{x : g^k(x) \in Y = [0,1]^2\ \forall k \in \Z\}$; itinerary map $\varphi(x)_j = a_j$ with $g^j(x) \in H_{a_j}$ is a homeomorphism conjugating $g|_X$ to the shift $\sigma$ on $\Sigma_2 = \{0,1\}^{\Z}$.

generalized horseshoe: $N$ strips + cone conditions $\Rightarrow$ shift on $N$ symbols

↓count orbits symbolically

Consequences

  • countably many (saddle) periodic orbits, of every period
  • uncountably many aperiodic orbits; a dense orbit
  • $X$ is a hyperbolic set $\Rightarrow$ the whole picture persists under perturbation
  • $g|_X$ is chaotic; Lyapunov exponents $\ln\mu > 0 > \ln\lambda$

alternative entrance: Shilnikov saddle-focus loop (zoo) also forces horseshoes

The bifurcation zoo §8, §12, §13

Bifurcation loss of equivalence under parameter variation

Families $x' = f(x,p)$, $y' = g(y,q)$ are fiber topologically equivalent if a homeomorphism $\xi(p) = q$ matches parameters and each $f(\cdot, p) \sim g(\cdot, \xi(p))$ topologically. A bifurcation diagram stratifies parameter space by equivalence class; an unfolding contains the bifurcation persistently; the minimal number of parameters is the codimension.

Homological equation

$L : H_k \to H_k$ on homogeneous vector polynomials, $\;L h = Dh \cdot Ay - A\,h(y) = [\,h, Ay\,]$.

Solvable part of $f^{(k)}$ is removed; resonant terms span a complement $R_k = \ker L^*$ of $\operatorname{range} L$ (if no generalized $0$-eigenvectors: $H_k = \ker L \oplus \operatorname{range} L$).

in practice: write $L$ as matrix $M^{(k)}$ in monomial basis, e.g. $k=2$: $\{x^2, xy, y^2\} \otimes \{\partial_x, \partial_y\} \cong \R^6$; resonant terms from $\ker (M^{(k)})^{\!\top}$

Jets & determinacy §11

$k$-jet $J^k F$ = Taylor polynomial to order $k$. BT 2-jet $y_1' = y_2$, $y_2' = y_1^2 \pm y_1 y_2$ is topologically determinate: blow-up $\Phi(\theta, r) = (r\cos\theta, r\sin\theta)$, desingularize $\bar F = r^{-l}(D\Phi^{-1} \circ F \circ \Phi)$ — the origin blows up to a circle of hyperbolic equilibria, so Hartman–Grobman finishes the classification.

Renormalization & universality §17

Idea

act on maps by rescaling phase space, time (iterate), and parameters; self-similar dynamics = fixed point of the renormalization operator.

fold: $g^2(y;q) \approx 2q + y - 2y^2$ rescales back to a fold ($\alpha = 2$, $\beta = 4$) $\Rightarrow$ passage time $N(q) \sim q^{-1/2}$ (critical slowing down)

Feigenbaum universality S-unimodal maps

flip cascade $p_0 < p_1 < \dots \to p_\infty$ (logistic: $p_0 = 3$, 2-cycle multiplier $4 + 2p - p^2$),

$\delta_F = \lim_n \frac{p_n - p_{n-1}}{p_{n+1} - p_n} \approx 4.6692, \qquad d_n = \kappa_2\, \alpha_F^{-n},\ \ \alpha_F \approx -2.5029$

$d_n$: distance of the superstable $2^n$-cycle to the critical point $x = \tfrac12$ (superstable: all multipliers $= 0$, i.e. $\tfrac12$ in the cycle)

fixed-point equation $g(x) = \alpha\, g^2(x/\alpha)$, $g(0)=1$, $g'(0)=0$; $\delta_F$ = the unique unstable eigenvalue of the linearized operator — the same constants for every such family.

Conley homeo of compact metric $X$

$\exists$ complete Lyapunov function $L \in C(X,\R)$:

  • (T1) $L(g(x)) < L(x)$ off $\CR(g)$
  • (T2) on $\CR(g)$: $L(x) = L(y) \iff x, y$ chain equivalent

Construction via attractor–repellor pairs

dual repellor $A^* = \bigcap_{k \ge 0} g^{-k}(X \setminus \overline U)$ of an attractor $A$ with isolating $U$; there is $L_i \in C(X, [0,1])$ with $L_i|_{A_i} = 0$, $L_i|_{A_i^*} = 1$, strictly decreasing elsewhere. Then

$\CR(g) = \bigcap_i \left( A_i \cup A_i^* \right), \qquad L = \sum_i \tfrac{2}{3^i} L_i$

$L(\CR)$ lands in the middle-third Cantor set — hence nowhere dense

Ergodic theorems

Krylov–Bogolyubov

$X$ compact metric, $g$ continuous $\Rightarrow$ an invariant Borel probability measure exists (empirical averages + Riesz representation)

Gauss map invariant measures can be exotic

$g(x) = \tfrac1x - \lfloor \tfrac1x \rfloor$ on $[0,1)$ preserves $\mu((a,b)) = \tfrac{1}{\ln 2}\int_a^b \tfrac{dy}{1+y}$ — the shift on continued-fraction digits.

Poincaré recurrence

$g$ measure-preserving, $\mu(B) > 0$ $\Rightarrow$ a.e. $x \in B$ returns to $B$ infinitely often.

Birkhoff $f \in L^1$

$\frac1n \sum_{j=0}^{n-1} f(g^j(x)) \longrightarrow f^*(x)$  a.e.

$f^*$ invariant, $\int f^* d\mu = \int f d\mu$;  ergodic case: $f^* = \int f\, d\mu$ a.e. — time average = space average.

von Neumann: also $S_n f \to f^*$ in $L^p$; key lemma: maximal ergodic theorem $\int_{\{F_N > 0\}} f\, d\mu \ge 0$

Koopman & Perron–Frobenius operators

$L_g f = f \circ g$ on $L^p$ (isometry); adjoint $P_g$: $\langle P f, h \rangle = \langle f, L h \rangle$.

  • ergodic $\iff 1$ is a simple eigenvalue of $L_g$
  • invertible + continuous spectrum ($1$ the only eigenvalue) $\Rightarrow$ weak mixing
  • strong mixing $\iff \langle P^n f, h \rangle \to \langle f, 1 \rangle \langle 1, h \rangle$

irrational rotation: eigenfunctions $e^{2\pi i n x}$ span $L^2$ (pure point) $\Rightarrow$ not weak mixing

Entropy §23

Entropy of a partition → of a map

$H(\mathcal{A}) = -\sum_j \mu(A_j) \ln \mu(A_j)$
$h(g, \mathcal{A}) = \lim_n \tfrac1n H\Big(\bigvee_{j=0}^{n-1} g^{-j} \mathcal{A}\Big), \qquad h(g) = \sup_{\mathcal{A}} h(g, \mathcal{A})$

$\mathcal{A} \vee \mathcal{B} = \{A_k \cap B_n\}$: joint refinement; $H(\mathcal{A} \vee \mathcal{B}) = H(\mathcal{A}) + H(\mathcal{B} \mid \mathcal{A})$

Kolmogorov–Sinai  &  invariance

$h$ is a conjugacy invariant; for a generating partition $\mathcal{A}$: $h(g) = h(g, \mathcal{A})$.

Conditional entropy & rules

$H(\mathcal{A} \mid \mathcal{B}) = -\sum_{i,j} \mu(B_j)\, \mu(A_i \mid B_j) \ln \mu(A_i \mid B_j)$

  • $H(\mathcal{A} \vee \mathcal{B}) = H(\mathcal{A}) + H(\mathcal{B} \mid \mathcal{A})$;  $\mathcal{A} \subseteq \mathcal{B} \Rightarrow H(\mathcal{A}) \le H(\mathcal{B})$
  • $h(g, \mathcal{A}) \le H(\mathcal{A})$;  $h(g, \mathcal{A}) \le h(g, \mathcal{B}) + H(\mathcal{A} \mid \mathcal{B})$
  • $g$-invariance: $H(g^{-1}\mathcal{A} \mid g^{-1}\mathcal{B}) = H(\mathcal{A} \mid \mathcal{B})$

Why entropy is finer than spectrum

Bernoulli shifts: $h = -\sum p_j \ln p_j$;  $p = (\tfrac12, \tfrac12)$: $h = \ln 2$,  $p = (\tfrac13,\tfrac13,\tfrac13)$: $h = \ln 3$ $\Rightarrow$ not conjugate, though spectrally indistinguishable.

entropy $\approx$ average information produced per iteration
